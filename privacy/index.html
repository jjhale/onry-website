Onry

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Onry ("Onry", "we", "us") is a mobile app that helps caregivers log and share events about the people they care for ("care recipients"). This policy explains what information Onry collects, how it's used, who can see it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Onry collects the information you and your care team enter into the app, including:

  • Account information — your email address and password, used to create and sign in to your account, the name you give yourself in the app and, if you add one, your profile photo. Passwords are stored only in hashed form.
  • Care recipient information — the name and, if you choose to add them, the date of birth and photo of each care recipient you set up. Photos are cropped, shrunk and re-encoded on your phone before they're uploaded, which removes the data a camera stores in a photo, such as where it was taken. Onry uses the camera only when you take one of these photos. If you choose one from your library instead, iOS gives Onry only that photo, not access to your library.
  • Care team information — who is on each care recipient's care team and whether they are an admin or a supporter, who owns each care team, the invite links admins create (with an optional note about who each link is for), and requests to join a care team.
  • Event data — the care events you and your care team log (for example, notes, categories and times), who logged each one, and the list of event types for each care recipient. This includes events the app records for you, such as how you responded to a prompt (for example, whether it was done, snoozed, skipped or missed).
  • Event history — if an event is changed or removed, we keep a copy of the earlier version, so that a care recipient's record can't be lost or quietly rewritten.
  • Places — the named places a care recipient spends time at (for example Home or School), each with a time zone, and which place the care recipient was at, and when. A place is stored only as the name your care team gives it, not as an address or a position on a map.
  • Prompts — the prompts and to-dos an admin sets up for a care recipient (for example a toilet reminder), how important each one is, and each person's answers.
  • Shifts — if an admin turns shifts on for a care recipient: the kinds of shift, the roster, when each person went on and off duty, when someone is out and when they expect to be back, and the checks and notes admins add to past shifts.

We don't collect analytics, advertising identifiers, or device tracking data, and your phone's location never reaches our servers.

Location and motion

On iPhone, Onry can use your phone's location to suggest which of the care recipient's places you're at when you log something. While you're on a shift, it can also ask whether the care recipient has arrived at or left one of their places. This is optional and off until you turn it on. Your location stays on your phone: our servers never receive coordinates, only which of the care recipient's places they're at, as if you had picked it yourself.

Who turns it on

  • An admin turns on Places from phones for each care recipient. While it's off, no phone on the care team uses its location for that care recipient.
  • You choose when your own phone is used, in Settings → This phone's location and motion: Never (the default), While I'm clocked in, During my rostered shifts or Always. This is set on your phone, and nobody else can change it. With During my rostered shifts, Onry watches the places all the time and ignores what it notices outside your shifts, so iOS may still show that Onry used your location then.
  • iOS asks your permission first: location "While Using" to suggest places, "Always" to notice when your phone arrives at or leaves a place while Onry is closed, and Motion & Fitness. You can change these in iOS Settings at any time.

What stays on your phone

  • A map of the care recipient's places, learned from where your phone was when you tapped I'm here now, or logged a place while you were on a shift. It's kept in your phone's secure storage, isn't included in iCloud backups or moved to a new phone, and is deleted when you sign out, leave the care team, or an admin turns Places from phones off. You can see which places your phone has learned, and forget them, in the app.
  • Every location reading. iOS tells Onry when your phone arrives at or leaves one of the care recipient's places (at most 20 places in all), and Onry uses your current location to suggest which place you're at when you log something.
  • Your motion history, which iOS records (for example walking, driving or still). Onry reads it on your phone to tell whether you were carrying your phone when it arrived or left, so that it doesn't ask about a phone left in a bag or a car. It's never sent to us.

What gets recorded

Nothing about a care recipient's location is recorded unless you choose it:

  • Suggestions. When you log something, Onry may mark the place you're at as Nearby. It's only saved if you pick it and save, as if you had picked it yourself.
  • Arrived? and Left? questions. Only while you're on a shift with the care recipient that you started in the app, your phone may ask "Arrived at School?" or "Left School?". If you tap Log it, Onry records that the care recipient is at that place, or on the move, at the time your phone arrived or left, and marks the record as logged from a phone. If you ignore the question, nothing is recorded. At other times, such as on your commute or while someone else is looking after them, Onry asks nothing and records nothing about where your phone goes.

Location and motion features aren't available on Android.

Information stored on your device

Onry keeps your sign-in session and the map of the care recipient's places in your device's secure storage. Your notification settings (such as which prompts to notify you about), when your phone may be used for location and motion, and the care recipient you last selected are stored on your device only.

Notifications

Prompts, and the Arrived? and Left? questions, are notifications your own phone schedules. Onry also sends notifications from our servers when someone asks to join a care team you manage or joins it, and when your own request to join is approved or declined. You can turn these off with Notifications from other people in Settings. To reach your phone, we keep the notification address Apple gives it. We remove it when you sign out on that phone or turn these notifications off, and when your account is deleted. Notifications pass through Expo and Apple, so their text never names anyone or says anything about a care recipient. It only says what kind of thing happened, and the app shows the details.

How we use your information

Information you provide is used only to operate the app for you and your care team:

  • To create your account and authenticate you.
  • To store and display the events and care team data you and your care team enter.
  • To share event data with the people an admin has added to a care recipient's care team.
  • To limit how quickly certain actions can be repeated (such as creating invite links or logging events), which protects your data if an account is misused.
  • To respond to you when you contact us.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who can see your data

Data about a care recipient is visible only to the members of that care recipient's care team. Access is enforced at the database level, so a caregiver can only see care recipients whose team they have joined.

  • Admins can see the care recipient's full event history, including earlier versions of changed or removed events.
  • Supporters can see events logged in the last 7 days, plus any events they logged themselves.
  • Places, including those logged from a phone, are events, so they're visible to the care team in the same way.
  • Shifts — admins can see everyone's shifts and time on duty. Supporters can see their own, and the names of the people on duty with the care recipient now, but not anyone else's kind of shift or hours.
  • Your name and profile photo are visible to the other members of every care team you're on.
  • Joining a care team — invite links grant nothing by themselves. When you use one, the care recipient's admins see your name and email address so they can decide whether to approve you. You can't see anything about the care recipient until you're approved. Invite links work once and expire after 7 days, and we store only a fingerprint of each link, never the link itself.

If you leave a care team, or an admin removes you, you lose access to that care recipient. The events you logged stay on the care recipient's record for the rest of the team.

Service providers

We use a small number of service providers to run Onry:

  • Supabase hosts our database and photos, and handles authentication.
  • Expo delivers our notifications to your phone through the Apple Push Notification service. It receives each notification's text and your phone's notification address. Expo also delivers app updates, so it sees your phone's IP address and which version of Onry it has when the app checks for one.
  • Cloudflare R2 stores our nightly database backups. Backups are encrypted before they leave our systems, and Cloudflare cannot read them.
  • Formspree receives messages you send through our contact form.

We don't share your data with anyone else.

Deleting your data

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app, from Settings → Delete Account. If you're the owner of any care teams, you first choose for each one whether to hand ownership to another admin or to delete that care recipient's record along with your account.

Your account is deleted 30 days after you ask. Straight away, you lose access to your care teams, your teammates see you as leaving the team, your open invite links stop working and your pending join requests are withdrawn. During those 30 days you can sign in and restore your account. After 30 days your name, profile photo, settings, email address, password and notification addresses are deleted permanently. The events you logged are kept, because they are part of each care recipient's record. They are shown with a label such as "Former carer 1" instead of your name, which an admin of that care recipient can rename.

Deleting a care recipient's record

The owner of a care team can delete the care recipient's record in the app. It disappears for everyone on the care team straight away, and the owner can restore it for 30 days. After 30 days the care recipient's record and everything recorded about them (their photo, events, event history, event types, places, shifts and roster, prompts, care team, invite links and join requests) are deleted permanently.

Backups and retention

We keep your data for as long as your account or the care recipient's record is active, and delete it as described above. Encrypted backups are kept so we can recover from a failure: daily backups for 30 days, weekly backups for 120 days, and monthly backups for one year. Deleted data can remain in these backups until they expire. Backups are only used to restore the service after a failure. If we ever restore one, we'll tell you, because a deletion you asked for after the backup was made may need to be asked for again.

If you can't use the in-app options, or want something else deleted, contact us.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit between the app and our servers. Access to every care recipient's data is checked by the database itself for each request, not just by the app. You can sign out of every device at once from Settings if you lose a phone or signed in on one that isn't yours. Signing out on a phone also deletes its map of the care recipient's places and stops it noticing when it arrives at or leaves them.

Children's privacy

Onry is intended for use by caregivers (adults). A care recipient may be a child or other dependent, but their information is entered and controlled by their caregivers, not by the care recipient directly.
